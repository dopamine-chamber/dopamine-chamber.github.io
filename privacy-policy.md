# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 10, 2026*

This website, **the handstand dilemma**, is a personal blog about the physics of handstand balance. It does not display advertising, and I do not sell your personal data or share it for marketing purposes. This page explains what data is processed when you visit the site or write to me, why, and what rights you have.

---

### Data Controller

The data controller is Benedetta Giglio, based in Turin, Italy. For any question about this policy or your data, write to benedettagiglio@gmail.com.

---

### Data We Collect

I do not ask you to create an account or to provide personal information to read this website. Data may be processed in three ways.

**Automatically, when you visit the website:**

- IP address and technical request data
- Device and browser information
- Pages visited and time spent on the site
- Approximate location (country or city level)
- Online identifiers stored in analytics cookies, only if you consent

**When you write to me:** your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message.

**When you ask to join the newsletter:** your email address and, if you include it, your name.

---

### Why I Use Your Data

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or regions with equivalent laws, your data is processed on the legal bases below.

| Purpose | Legal basis | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding how the website is used (Google Analytics) | Consent | Up to 14 months, as configured in Google Analytics |
| Hosting, security and technical operation of the website, including the delivery of its code libraries | Legitimate interest | According to the providers' own policies |
| Replying to your emails | Legitimate interest | As long as needed to handle your request |
| Newsletter list | Consent | Until you withdraw your consent or ask for deletion |

Legitimate interest here means keeping the website secure and working properly, and answering the messages you send me.

---

### Cookies

This website uses analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it (for example, which pages are read and from which country visitors come), so that I can improve its content. These cookies are installed only after you give your consent through the cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the cookie settings on this website. Your choice is stored on your device so that you are not asked again every time.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes users | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Maintains session state | 2 years |

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings, or install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Refusing or blocking cookies does not prevent you from reading the website.

This website does not use cookies for advertising or profiling.

---

### Newsletter

The newsletter has not been launched yet. If you ask to join the list by emailing me, I collect your email address and, if you include it, your name. I use them only to tell you when the newsletter starts and, afterwards, to send it to you. The list is kept in my personal email account (Gmail) and I do not share it with anyone. If I decide to use a dedicated email service in the future, I will update this policy before doing so.

You can withdraw your consent and ask me to delete your address at any time, and every newsletter will explain how to unsubscribe. If the newsletter is never launched, I will delete all the addresses collected.

---

These services may collect information directly from your browser or device, and are governed by their own privacy policies:

- **Google Analytics**, to measure website traffic (only with your consent): [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Gmail (Google)**, which hosts the email account I use to receive messages and newsletter requests: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### International Data Transfers

Some of these providers, such as Google and GitHub, are based in the United States or may process data outside the EEA. Where this happens, transfers rely on the safeguards described in their own policies, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

---

### Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Request restriction of the processing.
- Receive your data in a portable format.
- Object to the processing.
- Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before.

To exercise any of these rights, write to the address above. I will reply as soon as I can and in any case within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)), or with the authority of your own country.

---

### Children

This website is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect their personal data.

---

### Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the latest revision is shown at the top of this page.

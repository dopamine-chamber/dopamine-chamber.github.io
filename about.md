I'm just a handstand practitioner, obsessed with the art of handbalancing. I'm a '90s kid: *blogging is my love language*. This project is a deep-dive experiment into the study of the scientific principles behind handstands, or at least the models we use to describe it. Years of observing the handstand phenomenon, broken down into the 'why' and the 'how'. 

I joined the AdSense ad program to help fund this project. *Disassembling handstands* requires a lot of effort, research, and editorial work, as well as a significant amount of time. To maintain an ethical and honest approach, ads will always be placed at the very end of the article, so they only appear if the post has kept the reader engaged all the way to the bottom.

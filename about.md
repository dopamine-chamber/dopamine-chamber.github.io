I’ve been practicing handstands for about six years, and in that time I’ve come to realize how good it feels to channel my thinking into something so all-consuming. The Handstand Dilemma was born because my practice had grown too intricate for these thoughts to stay inside my head. I wanted to share them and build something out of them.

I’m a ’90s kid: blogging is my love language, and writing is one of the few things I’ve always been good at. Surprisingly, I’m a terribly introverted person, and sharing so much with strangers online is something I’ve always found almost therapeutic. With this project I wanted many parts of myself to meet: the kid who spends hours writing for her blog, the practitioner, the nerd, the need to have answers and to put things in order. This project holds what I’ve understood about handstands.

The editorial flow is a reorganization of my stream of consciousness, whose complexity grows in step with the time I spend practicing. Each topic is explored based on what the scientific literature and reliable sources tell us about handstand practice, so that personal experience and science communication can come together.
